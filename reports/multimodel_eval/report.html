ForensIQ Multi-Model Evaluation

Isolation Forest vs. Local Outlier Factor vs. One-Class SVM, equal review budget (top 5% of files). All values computed by multimodel_eval.py from the extracted artifacts.

1. Scope

  • Images evaluated: Image 1 (XP Hacking, 11,773 files) and Image 2 (Win7 Data Leakage, 85,017 files).
  • Features per file: Image 1: 22, Image 2: 22 of the 23-feature design. delta_si_fn requires $FILE_NAME timestamps that the stored MFT data does not contain, so it is excluded.
  • Not evaluated:
    • img3: extracted artifacts not available for this image
  • One-Class SVM is trained on a 10,000-file random subsample and scores every file; LOF uses 20 neighbours.
  • A target is "caught" when any of its files is in the flagged set. Image 1 has 7 target families (15 files); Image 2 has 19 targets (38 files).
